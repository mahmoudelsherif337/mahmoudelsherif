Payments · Team and structure

Rebuilding the Payments team around clear roles

When I joined Payments in mid-2026, most of the team were new to the bank, only two people knew every task, and follow-ups competed with the daily payment rush.

  • Hired 7 people, including 2 Assistant Managers.
  • Split the unit into two teams: one processes payments, the other handles control, reconciliation and inquiries.
  • Agreed payment cut-off times with the business; set maker and checker finish times per processing task.
  • Set up a checklist with a named maker, checker and backup for every task.

Payments · Nostro follow-up

Reducing aged Nostro items by 95%

In July the Payments unit had a large backlog of aged Nostro items, some older than 15 days. Follow-up was competing with daily processing, so items kept ageing.

  • Nostro follow-up was given to one team with a clear owner.
  • Open items are followed up daily, and items blocked outside the unit are escalated.
  • By October the backlog was down by about 95%, and around 90% of the remaining items were waiting on Compliance.

Payment tools

Tools I built for the Payments team. Screens show sample data.

Email workflow · Microsoft Access + Outlook

Making sure every payment email has an owner

The payment group receives around 400–500 emails a day, many of them payment requests. Emails were sometimes missed, so I built a workflow on top of Outlook that tracks each one from arrival to close.

  • Every email gets a reference, a named owner and a 60-minute reply deadline.
  • Unanswered emails escalate automatically to the manager and supervisors.
  • Classification is required before closing, giving volumes and processing time per email type.
  • A daily control proves every email in Outlook is in the workflow.
Bulk upload control · Excel VBA

Controlled bulk uploads with three checks

Bulk upload files used to be edited by hand, which led to a wrong-amount posting and audit comments. These tools now cover internal-transfer (Flexcube) and SWIFT outward (MT103 via RTGS) uploads.

  • The checker re-enters the request independently; the tool matches line by line.
  • Built-in checks for balance, field formats and possible duplicate transfers.
  • Only the tool creates the upload files, after the check passes; the batch then locks.
  • A separate second-checker tool gives the unit head the final review before upload.

Daily controls in three layers

We applied the Egypt Operations Control Enhancement Program in Payments and added payment controls for the issues we had seen before: duplicate payments, wrong beneficiaries and wrong upload amounts.

Cards · MasterCard settlement tool · Excel VBA

MasterCard settlement prepared from the network files

I built this tool to remove manual settlement steps. The tool loads the MasterCard T140 files, generates grand totals by transaction type for debit and credit cards in USD and EGP, separates transactions from interchange, reconciles against the core banking report and creates the upload sheet.

  • Three-step flow: upload T140 files, generate totals, upload the CBS report.
  • Control cells must equal zero before the upload sheet can be used.
Cards · GL mapping

Rebuilding the GL mapping for all card transactions

A review of the cards GLs showed that many transaction types were mapped to the wrong GLs. For example, VISA POS transactions were routed to EBC GLs and EBC transactions to VISA GLs, and there were many similar cases across networks and transaction types.

With mixed sources in the same GLs, balances often ended the day against their nature, settlement needed manual intervention, and breaks were hard to trace.

  • Reviewed the mapping of every card transaction type, across all networks.
  • Rebuilt the mapping so each network and transaction type posts to its own correct GL.
  • Created new GLs with the right payable and receivable mapping.
  • Each GL now reconciles to a single source, which makes daily reconciliation simpler.
